Privacy policy

Valuables Vault · Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, Linux and web app · effective 9 October 2026 (version 0.9.1)

In short: Valuables Vault does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays encrypted on your own device. We have no server, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Who is responsible

Ing.-Büro Sachit Shrestha · e-mail support@medtec24.com

What the app stores, and where

The inventory you create — item descriptions, values, photos, documents, owners, witnesses, storage locations and bank-locker details — is stored only on the device where you enter it, encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The key is derived from your vault password (PBKDF2-SHA-256, 600,000 iterations); the password itself is never stored. Neither we nor anyone else can read your data, and it cannot be recovered without the password.

What the app sends over the internet

Your vault data is never sent. The only connections are to GitHub (GitHub, Inc.), which hosts the program:

  • Update check (can be switched off in Settings → Notifications & updates): about once a day the app asks GitHub for the latest version number, and downloads a new version only when you agree. The request contains no vault data and no identifier — only what any web request contains, such as your IP address.
  • Web app: the program files are downloaded from GitHub Pages.

GitHub may process your IP address as part of these requests, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. Every other network connection is blocked by the app's security policy.

Reminder notifications

Reminders (insurance renewal, backup due, bank-locker review, outdated valuations, expiring documents) are worked out on your device. So that they can appear while the vault is locked or the app is closed, the app keeps only the dates and the type of the next reminders outside the encrypted vault (on phones and tablets, in the system's notification scheduler). Notifications never contain names, insurers, amounts or other details. You can switch them off in Settings → Notifications & updates or in your device's settings.

Permissions

Backups and exports

Backups are encrypted files you save yourself (for example to a USB stick or your own cloud folder). Excel/CSV exports and printed reports are unencrypted by design; the app warns you before creating them. Where you keep these files is under your control.

Sharing, children, deletion

We do not share or sell data — we never receive any. The app is not directed at children. To delete all data, use Settings → Erase vault on this device or uninstall the app (on desktop, also delete the app data folder listed in the installation guide).

Your rights

Because we process no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For questions, contact support@medtec24.com. You may also complain to a data protection supervisory authority.

Changes

If the app ever starts processing data (for example optional sync through storage you host), this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Datenschutzerklärung (Kurzfassung)

Valuables Vault erhebt, überträgt und teilt keine personenbezogenen Daten. Alle Eingaben (Gegenstände, Fotos, Dokumente, Schließfachangaben) werden ausschließlich verschlüsselt (AES-256-GCM) auf Ihrem Gerät gespeichert. Es gibt keinen Server, keine Konten, keine Analyse- oder Werbedienste. Die App fragt etwa einmal täglich bei GitHub nach der neuesten Versionsnummer (abschaltbar); dabei werden keine Tresordaten übertragen. Erinnerungen werden auf dem Gerät berechnet; außerhalb des verschlüsselten Tresors werden nur Datum und Art der nächsten Erinnerungen gespeichert, nie Namen oder Beträge. Kamera und Dateizugriff werden nur genutzt, wenn Sie selbst ein Foto aufnehmen, einen QR-Code scannen oder eine Datei auswählen bzw. speichern. Die Web-App wird über GitHub Pages ausgeliefert; dabei kann GitHub Ihre IP-Adresse verarbeiten.
